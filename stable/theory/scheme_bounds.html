Voigt and Reuss bounds ​

Before this page

Homogenization schemes, where the concentration tensors and the average relations they satisfy are set out.

The two bounds use no shape, no reference medium and no Eshelby problem: they assume a uniform field throughout the RVE, the strain for one and the stress for the other. That crude assumption is what makes them rigorous, since a uniform field is admissible in the corresponding energy principle (Hill, 1952).
