Its mean gradients satisfy linear relations whose coefficients are Hill tensors of ellipsoids: no series is needed. This page derives them. 5. Scope ​

  • Isotropic layers, isotropic medium, perfect interfaces. An interface with a resistance or a surface conductance adds a polarization carried by the surface, which the relations of section 3 do not contain: the confocal spheroid of Layered spheroid treats it.

  • Conduction only. In elasticity the Green operator also involves the biharmonic potential of the ellipsoid, whose exterior values are not in the ratio of the volumes of confocal ellipsoids: the relation of section 3 is then an approximation, measured in Coated inclusions: closures on the mean strains, and the exact solution.

  • Confocal boundaries. Similar ellipsoids are not confocal; for them, as for any other shape of the layers, the response is computed on a finite-element cell (Finite-element inclusions).

Where to go next ​

The ellipsoid, the crack and the layered patterns are now solved. The next chapter keeps the single heterogeneity and leaves the ellipsoid: Toroidal inclusions and Holes given by a conformal map are two shapes with an exact solution, and Approximation by Gaussians opens the treatment of any shape.
