The multipole expansion method for ellipsoids ​

Before this page

The multipole expansion method for spheroids, whose structure is kept here: the response of a particle over its own harmonics, the translation by projection on the receiving surface, the far images of a lattice through the spherical harmonics.

The particles are now ellipsoids of three different semi-axes, all oriented alike, with a perfect or a Kapitza interface, in an isotropic matrix of conductivity (Kushch, 2020). The harmonics adapted to an ellipsoid are those of Lamé, built here from the theory of Dassios (2012). An assembly of Ellipsoids, or of InterfacedEllipsoids for an imperfect interface, selects this page.

4. Orientation and effective conductivity ​

The ellipsoids of an assembly share their orientation, their axes along those of the assembly; the problem is solved in the frame of their axes ordered by decreasing semi-axis, and the effective conductivity turned back to the frame of the assembly. The dipole of a particle is read on its harmonics of degree 1,     , and the formulas of the spheres apply. The constituents are isotropic; anisotropic ones (Kushch et al., 2017) and the elasticity of ellipsoids are listed in the roadmap.
