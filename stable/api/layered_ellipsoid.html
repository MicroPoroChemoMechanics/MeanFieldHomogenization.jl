API — LayeredEllipsoid ​

layer_count, layer_modulus and layer_volume_fraction are the generics of LayeredSpheres, layer_semiaxes and outer_semiaxes those of LayeredSpheroids: see API — LayeredSphere and API — LayeredSpheroid for their docstrings.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredEllipsoids Module
julia
MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredEllipsoids

Isotropic n-layer inclusion bounded by confocal ellipsoids of three unequal axes (a core and its confocal coatings), in conduction, in an isotropic medium, with perfect interfaces. Public entry points: LayeredEllipsoid, layered_ellipsoid_from_fractions.

Like the other layered inclusions it has no Hill tensor and enters the schemes through its concentration tensors. They need no series: the mean gradients of the layers satisfy as many linear relations as there are layers, which are exact for confocal boundaries (conductivity.jl), and whose coefficients are the Hill tensors of the ellipsoids, taken from Elasticity.hill_tensor.

  • geometry.jl : the type, its constructors and its accessors.

  • conductivity.jl : the relations between the mean gradients, the concentration tensors and the entries of the schemes.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredEllipsoids.LayeredEllipsoid Type
julia
LayeredEllipsoid(semi_axes, conductivities; basis)

An inclusion of N isotropic layers bounded by confocal ellipsoids, for the conduction problem. semi_axes[k] is the triplet of semi-axes of the outer boundary of the layer k, along the three vectors of basis (the canonical basis by default); conductivities[k] is the conductivity of the layer k, a TensISO{2,3}. The first layer is the core.

The boundaries must be confocal: the squares of the semi-axes of two of them differ by the same amount along the three axes, and increase outward. This is checked on numbers and taken as given on symbols. layered_ellipsoid_from_fractions builds the family from the core and the volume fractions.

The interfaces are perfect, and the surrounding medium is isotropic. A sphere or a spheroid is the particular case of equal semi-axes, also served, with imperfect interfaces, by LayeredSphere and LayeredSpheroid.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredEllipsoids.layered_ellipsoid_from_fractions Function
julia
layered_ellipsoid_from_fractions(core_axes, fractions, conductivities; basis) -> LayeredEllipsoid

The confocal layered ellipsoid whose core has the semi-axes core_axes and whose layers have the volume fractions fractions in the inclusion (their sum is 1; fractions[1] is that of the core).
