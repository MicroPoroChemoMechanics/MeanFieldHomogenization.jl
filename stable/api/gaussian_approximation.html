API — Arbitrary shapes by Gaussian approximation ​

Cracks of arbitrary contour and heterogeneities of arbitrary shape, by the Gaussian approximation of their integral equations: see Approximation by Gaussians, Planar cracks of any contour and Heterogeneities of any shape for the theory, and the manual pages (cracks, heterogeneities) for the usage. The crack functions (cod_tensor, sif, the contributions) are those of API — Cracks.

MeanFieldHomogenization.GaussianApproximation Module
julia
GaussianApproximation

Cracks and heterogeneities of arbitrary shape, by the approximation of Maz'ya (Maz'ya and Schmidt, 1996): the unknown field of an integral equation is expanded on Gaussians centered at the nodes of a regular grid. The action of the integral operator on a Gaussian is known in closed form, the matrix is a convolution evaluated by FFT, and a shape enters only through the nodes it contains — no mesh.

  • AbstractRegion: describing a shape (catalog, set operations, function, image);

  • PlanarCrack: a flat crack of any contour, accepted by every scheme;

  • quasi_interpolate, saturation_error: the approximation itself.

MeanFieldHomogenization.GaussianApproximation.crack_opening Function
julia
crack_opening(crack::PlanarCrack, C₀, Σ; K_interface=nothing) -> (points, openings)
crack_opening(crack::PlanarCrack, K₀, σ; α_interface=nothing) -> (points, jumps)

Opening of the crack under the remote stress Σ (or jump of the potential under the remote   ), on its finest grid: the positions of the nodes in the plane of the crack, and at each node the displacement jump as its three components in the frame of the crack (a scalar in conduction).

The jump is the sum of the Gaussians taken at the node, not the coefficient of the Gaussian centered there: the two differ next to the edge of the crack and of its contacts, where the coefficients are not smooth.

The values that crack_opening returns at the nodes of the crack, laid on its grid: the coordinates x and y of the lines of nodes in the plane of the crack and the matrix Z[i, j] of the value at (x[i], y[j]), zero where the faces do not open. For a surface plot.

MeanFieldHomogenization.GaussianApproximation.edge_rule Function
julia
edge_rule(H) -> (γ, c₀)

Constants of the edge rule of a Gaussian crack of shape parameter H: the spring coefficient γ for which the edge of the discrete crack follows the edge of the region as it is swept across a cell, and the constant distance c₀, in grid steps, by which it then lies beyond it. Calibrated on a line crack, the local problem of any smooth edge, and memoized.

MeanFieldHomogenization.GaussianApproximation.gaussian_solve Function
julia
gaussian_solve(kernel::SampledKernel, inside, R; method=:cg, rtol=1e-10, itmax=0) -> Matrix

Solution W of the Gaussian system for each column of the right-hand sides R (m components per node kept, component after component). The kernel may carry ForwardDiff duals, nested or not: the derivatives of W are then exact for the discrete system, by the implicit-function theorem.

method is :cg (conjugate gradient with a block-Jacobi preconditioner, for a positive definite matrix, as that of a crack), :minres (symmetric, of any sign) or :gmres. itmax = 0 leaves the limit to the solver.
