API — LayeredCylinder ​

The layered cylinder shares its interfaces, its layer accessors and its pointwise entry points with the layered sphere: the functions listed on API — LayeredSphere have a method for a LayeredCylinder. This page lists what is its own.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders Module
julia
MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders

Infinite n-layer cylinder of circular section (core and coaxial coatings) embedded in an infinite matrix, with layers and matrix isotropic or transversely isotropic about the cylinder axis, and perfect or imperfect interfaces (spring, surface-elastic, Kapitza, surface-conductive). Public entry point: LayeredCylinder.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders.LayeredCylinder Type
julia
LayeredCylinder{T, N, Cs, Is, A} <: AbstractLayeredInclusion{3, T}

Infinite cylinder of circular section made of N coaxial layers (core and coatings), embedded in an infinite matrix. Each layer is isotropic or transversely isotropic about the cylinder axis, and so is the matrix: the problem then splits into four radial problems solved in closed form (see the theory page).

  • radii: , ascending; layer lies between and , with  .

  • moduli: one property per layer, a stiffness (TensISO{4,3} or a TensTI{4} whose axis is the cylinder axis) or a conductivity (TensISO{2,3} or TensTI{2}).

  • interfaces: the condition at each (PerfectInterface by default); interface is the boundary with the matrix.

  • axis: unit vector of the cylinder axis, by default.

Type parameters: T is the element type of the radii (Float64, BigFloat, ForwardDiff.Dual, Symbolics.Num, SymPyPythonCall.Sym), N the number of layers, A the element type of the axis.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders.LayeredCylinderFields Type
julia
LayeredCylinderFields(cyl, C₀)

Pointwise elastic solution of a LayeredCylinder in the reference C₀ (isotropic, or transversely isotropic about the axis), under a unit remote strain: the amplitudes of the radial problems in every layer and in the matrix. Build it once for many points; every local_* entry point also takes (cyl, C₀) and then solves the problem at each call.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders.LayeredCylinderTransportFields Type
julia
LayeredCylinderTransportFields(cyl, K₀)

Pointwise conduction solution of a LayeredCylinder in the reference K₀, under a unit remote gradient: conduction twin of LayeredCylinderFields.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders.cylinder_gradient_average Function
julia
cylinder_gradient_average(cyl, P₀, X∞)
cylinder_flux_average(cyl, P₀, X∞)

Averages over the material of the whole cylinder,   : they leave out the opening of the spring (or Kapitza) interfaces and the surface stress (or flux) of the membranes (or conducting surfaces), which the tensors of the schemes, gradient_gradient_concentration(cyl, P₁, P₀) and flux_gradient_concentration(cyl, P₁, P₀), include. Names in elasticity: cylinder_strain_average and cylinder_stress_average.

MeanFieldHomogenization.LayeredCylinders.cylinder_flux_average Function
julia
cylinder_flux_average(cyl, P₀, X∞)

Average over the material of the whole cylinder of the stress under the remote strain X∞, or of    under the remote gradient X∞:   , without the surface stress (or flux) of the membranes (or conducting surfaces), which flux_gradient_concentration(cyl, P₁, P₀) includes. Name in elasticity: cylinder_stress_average.

julia
cylinder_strain_average(args...; kw...)
