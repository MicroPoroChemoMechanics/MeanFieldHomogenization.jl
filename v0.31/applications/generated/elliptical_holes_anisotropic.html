Elliptical holes and cracks in an anisotropic plate ​

The whole page as one script

Every block of this page, in order, is scripts/111_elliptical_holes_anisotropic_plate.jl. From a clone of the repository, julia --project=docs -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()' once, then julia scripts/111_elliptical_holes_anisotropic_plate.jl, runs it end to end.

Before this page

The elliptic hole, from an isotropic to an anisotropic plate, which computes the stress around one hole, Plane problems for the cells of the plane, and Crack opening displacement and compliance for the tensors of a crack.

A plate of orthotropic material holds elliptical holes and cracks, without interaction. This page computes the compliance contribution of one hole and of one crack in such a plate, against the closed forms of Tsukrov and Kachanov, checks that an ellipse contributes as a circle plus two cracks, and recomputes the effective anisotropy of a plate with randomly oriented ellipses.

The stiffness of the plane is the inverse of that compliance, an orthotropic tensor of the plane in the axes of the plate (Plane problems):

julia
function plate(E₁, E₂, G₁₂, ν₁₂)
    ν₂₁ = ν₁₂ * E₂ / E₁
    d = 1 - ν₁₂ * ν₂₁
    return TensOrtho(E₁ / d, E₂ / d, ν₁₂ * E₂ / d, G₁₂, CanonicalBasis{2, Float64}())   ## C₁₁₁₁, C₂₂₂₂, C₁₁₂₂, C₁₂₁₂
end
const C₀ = plate(E₁, E₂, G₁₂, ν₁₂)
young_moduli(C) = (S = canonical_components(inv(C)); (1 / S[1, 1, 1, 1], 1 / S[2, 2, 2, 2]))
young_moduli(C₀)

function split_defect(a, b, φ, C)
    left = a * b * hole_H(ellipse(a, b, φ), C)
    right = a * b * hole_H(disk, C) + a * (a - b) * compliance_contribution(crack(φ), C) -
