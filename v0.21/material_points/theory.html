Integrating a law over a step ​

A nonlinear law is not a formula that turns a strain into a stress: the stress also depends on the history, carried by internal variables that evolve. A finite-element code, or a test machine, asks the law a discrete question: the gradients were at the beginning of a time step of length , the internal variables were ; the gradients are at its end; what are the fluxes, the internal variables, and how do the fluxes vary with ? This page derives the answer the code computes, and why it is exact.
