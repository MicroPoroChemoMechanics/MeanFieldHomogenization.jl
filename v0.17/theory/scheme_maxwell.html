Mori–Tanaka describes what surrounds an inclusion by the mean strain of the matrix alone. The Maxwell scheme keeps the matrix as reference medium and adds a second shape: that of a domain which holds the inclusions and which, filled with the effective medium, must disturb the matrix far away as the inclusions do. The argument rests on nothing but the far field of an inhomogeneity, which this page derives from the Green operator of the matrix (§1) before building the estimate on it (§2) and reading the concentration tensors off the construction (§3).

The notation is the intrinsic one of Barthélémy et al. (2019), who transpose the scheme to aging viscoelasticity. It is written here in elasticity, with the conventions of this documentation: is a compliance, and the Green operator of the matrix maps a polarization onto minus the strain it induces.

4. Relation to the other estimates ​

The estimate shares its reference medium with the dilute and Mori–Tanaka estimates, and its structure with the latter. The second form of the Mori–Tanaka estimate and the Maxwell estimate differ by a single term,

and they coincide when every inclusion phase has the Hill tensor of , whatever the moduli. A spherical domain therefore reproduces Mori–Tanaka for spherical inclusions only, and departs from it as soon as the inclusions are spheroids. Both concentration tensors then also coincide: the field of the cluster at infinity is the mean strain of the matrix.

The Ponte Castañeda–Willis estimate of the next page starts from entirely different premises, an energy principle and the two-point statistics of the inclusions, and yet returns the same tensor when a single ellipsoid describes the distribution of every pair of phases and has its shape. That page shows the coincidence; nothing in either derivation announces it.

5. In practice ​

The domain is a property of the microstructure, declared on the RVE and without default; the manual explains why (Distribution shape). The estimate is a closed form with no iteration, so that it accepts dual numbers and symbolic moduli like the dilute estimates, and conduction follows with in place of .
