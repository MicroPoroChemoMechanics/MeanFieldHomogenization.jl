The multipole expansion method for ellipsoids in elasticity ​

Before this page

The multipole expansion method for spheroids in elasticity, whose response by least squares, reading of close neighbors and spherical form are kept here, and the method for ellipsoids for the Lamé functions and the ellipsoidal harmonics.

The particles are ellipsoids of three different semi-axes   , aligned alike with the axes of the assembly, perfectly bonded, bonded by springs, or empty, in an isotropic matrix of shear modulus and Poisson ratio (Kushch, 2019; Kushch, 2019). As for spheroids, each one keeps the unknowns of a sphere; its own solutions are now built from the decaying ellipsoidal harmonics , degree 0 included.

It is kept at the degree 2 alone: there it brings the field of an ellipsoid under a uniform remote strain, Eshelby's, among the solutions of degree 2 and below, while at the other degrees it adds no accuracy and makes the four families nearly dependent. 2. Response of an ellipsoid ​

The ellipsoid receives the regular spherical solutions about its center and holds inside a series of regular spherical solutions for its Poisson ratio . On its surface, of unit normal , the traction is continuous and the displacement jumps by the compliance of the interface,

  and   the compliances of a SpringInterface of stiffnesses , , zero for a perfect bond; on a cavity the traction is zero. No symmetry keeps the order here: the conditions are imposed by least squares at the quadrature points of the surface of the method for ellipsoids. One ellipsoid in a domain of its own shape is Mori–Tanaka from  :

julia
using MeanFieldHomogenization, TensND, LinearAlgebra
iso(μ, ν) = TensISO{3}(2μ * (1 + ν) / (1 - 2ν), 2μ)                # (3κ, 2μ)
stiffness(asm, scheme) = TensND.mandel(homogenize(asm, scheme, :C), TensND.CanonicalBasis{3, Float64}())
semi = (1.5, 1.0, 0.5)
asm = ParticleAssembly(; boundary = MixedBC(Ellipsoid((semi ./ cbrt(0.5))...)))
add_matrix!(asm, Dict(:C => iso(1.0, 0.3)))
add_particle!(asm, :p, (0.0, 0.0, 0.0), Ellipsoid(semi...), Dict(:C => iso(100.0, 0.2)))
norm(stiffness(asm, MultipoleExpansion(; nmax = 2)) - stiffness(asm, MoriTanaka())) / norm(stiffness(asm, MoriTanaka()))
