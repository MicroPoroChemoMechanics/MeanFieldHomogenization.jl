On this page

Material laws ​

MaterialLaws is the part of MeanFieldHomogenization that deals with one material point: a constitutive law, linear or not, that may remember its history, and that may couple several fields. With it you can

  • write a law in a few lines of mathematics, either as the equations of its integration over a time step or as two potentials;

  • test it along any loading path, a tensile test, a creep test, a cycle, an undrained compression, before using it anywhere else;

  • plug it into a finite-element computation, exactly where a homogenized material plugs in today;

  • homogenize a composite of nonlinear phases into a law of the same kind.

The consistent tangent, the coupling blocks between fields and the derivatives with respect to the parameters are computed for you, by automatic differentiation: you never write a derivative by hand.

Four pieces that do not depend on each other ​

piecethe question it answerswhat you write
the lawwhat is the material?a ResidualLaw or a PotentialLaw: plain functions and a NamedTuple of parameters
the local algorithmhow is one step integrated?nothing: LocalNewton is the default
the loadingalong which path is the point driven?a LoadingPath, each component driven by its gradient or by its flux
the couplingwhere is the law evaluated?point_response for one point, material_response in an element loop

Any law runs with any algorithm, along any path, in any caller. Changing one piece never means touching another.

A first calculation ​

A steel bar pulled in tension, with von Mises plasticity and linear isotropic hardening. The law has five parameters; the path drives the axial strain from 0 to 0.5 % and leaves the lateral faces free.

julia
using MeanFieldHomogenization

law = J2Plasticity(; E = 200e3, ν = 0.3, σ₀ = 250.0, H = 2e3)     # MPa
path = LoadingPath(range(0, 1; length = 51); ε11 = t -> 5e-3 * t)
r = point_response(law, path)
r[:σ11][end], r[:p][end]      # axial stress (MPa) and cumulated plastic strain
(257.4257425742574, 0.003712871287128713)

r[:σ11] is the axial stress at every time of the path, r[:p] the cumulated plastic strain, an internal variable of the law. The lateral stresses r[:σ22] and r[:σ33] are zero, as the path asked: the driver found the lateral strains that make them vanish.

Three kinds of laws, one tool ​

kindwhat changesexample shipped
a law of the strain alonenothing is rememberedLinearElasticity, PowerLawElasticity
a law with internal variables and their evolutionthe state carries the history: plastic strain, viscous strain, damageJ2Plasticity, NortonViscoplasticity, MaxwellViscoelasticity
a law of coupled primal fieldsseveral gradients and their conjugate fluxes, with every coupling block of the tangentBiotPoroelasticity (strain and pore pressure)

The three are written the same way. A coupled law only lists its fields: gradients = (ε = 6, p = 1) and flux_names = (σ = 6, φ = 1) for poroelasticity; a thermo-hydro-mechanical law would add the temperature and the entropy in the same manner.

Reading order ​

Each law and each homogenized model has one page, which states the theory and runs it: the blocks of a page, in order, form a script of scripts/material_laws/ that runs on its own.

page

Secant methodsthe linear comparison composite, the classical and the modified secant methods; two power-law phases against the Voigt bound

Small strain

The laws are written in infinitesimal strain. The kinematics is a trait of the law (kinematics), so that finite strain can be added later without changing the laws written today.
