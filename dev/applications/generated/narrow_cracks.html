Narrow cracks: rings, rectangles, bands ​

The whole page as one script

Every block of this page, in order, is scripts/76_narrow_cracks.jl. From a clone of the repository, julia --project=docs -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()' once, then julia scripts/76_narrow_cracks.jl, runs it end to end.

Before this page

The manual page Planar cracks of any contour, and the theory page of the same name, §5, for the normalization of the compliance of a crack of any contour.

A crack much longer than wide opens, away from its ends, as a crack in plane strain of the same width. Pronina, Markov and Kachanov build on this a plane strain model of the compliance of narrow cracks, and correct it by factors fitted on computations for a ring and for a rectangle. This page computes the same cracks with PlanarCrack and sets the results against the model, against the published fits, and against the cracks for which the package has another solution: the annular crack and the ellipse.

julia

using TensND
using LinearAlgebra
using Printf
using Plots

The ring ​

A ring of radii   and  : half-width  , midline of length  , hence     . The paper corrects the model by a polynomial fitted on its computations. The package has the AnnularCrack, solved by a Galerkin method.

julia
plane_strain_ring(ξ) = π^2 / 2 * (1 - ξ)^2 * (1 + ξ)
y₁(ξ) = -0.0739ξ^3 + 0.2169ξ^2 - 0.2211ξ + 1.0778
ξs = 0.2:0.1:0.9

The rectangle ​

A rectangle of half-sides   and : the model gives    . The ends are not narrow, and what they take away from the model is the point of the comparison. The paper gives a fit of its computations in two pieces, and recalls two estimates by an equivalent circular crack: that of Budiansky and O'Connell, built on the area and the perimeter, and that of Fabrikant.

julia
y₂(λ) = λ ≤ 5 ? 8.208e-3 * λ^3 - 9.782e-2 * λ^2 + 3.998e-1 * λ + 0.231 :
    3.939e-6 * λ^3 - 3.032e-4 * λ^2 + 1.036e-2 * λ + 0.766
budiansky(λ) = 128λ^2 / (3π * (λ + 1)) / (4π * λ)
fabrikant(λ) = 32λ^2 / (3sqrt(λ^2 + 1)) / (4π * λ)

A band of any midline ​

The regions combine: a band along an arc of circle, with rounded or blunt ends, is a ring cut by a sector. The half-angle of the arc goes from a short band to the closed ring.

julia
function arc_band(R, r, α)
    sector = Region(x -> abs(atan(x[2], x[1])) ≤ α, (-R, -R), (R, R))
    return Annulus(R, r) ∩ sector
end
αs = (π / 6, π / 3, π / 2, 2π / 3, 5π / 6)
bands = [gaussian(arc_band(1.0, 0.7, α); n = 192) for α in αs]
for (α, v) in zip(αs, bands)
    model = 2π * 0.15^2 * (2α * 0.85)                 ## half-width 0.15, midline of radius 0.85
    @printf("2α = %3.0f°:  S B_N E' = %.4f   plane strain model %.4f   ratio %.3f\n", rad2deg(2α), v, model, v / model)
end
