Interacting cracks ​

Before this page

Crack opening displacement and compliance for the isolated crack, and Problems of the plane for the crack of a plane.

The schemes of a later chapter account for the other cracks through an average medium (Cracks seen as fractures). When a few cracks lie close to each other, the stress intensity factor at a tip depends on where exactly the others are: two collinear cracks amplify each other at their inner tips, two stacked cracks shield each other. This page solves the problem of straight cracks of a plane by the method of average tractions of Kachanov (1987), for an isotropic plane and, following Mauge and Kachanov (1994), for an anisotropic one.
